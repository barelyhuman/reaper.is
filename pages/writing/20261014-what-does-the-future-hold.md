I don't know if software is going to be dead or not, the whole web seems to think it will be though.

Anyways, tinytown.studio was meant to be a small focused app studio. Looking back, I wish I had put more effort into growing it into what I originally imagined. I didn't. The things that are running like Ping and Toggles aren't going anywhere. But I'm not sure what "app studio" means now when it takes a few prompts to clone whatever the hell I have built. I'm trying to figure out if tinytown has a place in whatever comes next. I don't have an answer yet.

It's a strange time to build things. I'll get back once I have better things to show.
